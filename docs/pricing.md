# Pricing

AdsWire is priced by the number of client accounts you manage — not by seat.
Your whole team is included in every plan.

| Plan | Client accounts | Monthly |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Up to 5 | $199 |
| Growth | Up to 20 | $599 |
| Agency | Up to 50 | $999 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | Contact us |

## Why per account, not per seat?

Agencies think in clients, not headcount. A plan that grows with your
client roster — not your team size — aligns cost with value.

The more clients you manage through AdsWire, the lower your per-account cost.

## 14-day free trial

All plans include a 14-day free trial. Full access, no credit card required.
If you do not convert, your account is suspended automatically at day 14.
