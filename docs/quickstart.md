# Quickstart

Connect AdsWire to your AI agent in under 5 minutes.
No card required. Google Ads access is live today.

## Step 1 — Create your account

[Start your free trial →](https://app.adswire.io/register)

14 days, full access, no credit card.

## Step 2 — Connect your AI agent

### Claude Desktop or claude.ai

1. Open Claude Desktop → **Customize** → **Connectors** → **+**
2. Select **Add custom connector**
3. Paste your MCP endpoint URL (shown in your AdsWire dashboard)
4. Click **Advanced settings** and enter your OAuth credentials
5. Click **Add** → Claude will show a **Connect** button
6. Click **Connect** → log in with your AdsWire account
7. Choose what your agent is allowed to do → click **Connect**

### Claude.ai (Team or Enterprise)

If you are on a Team or Enterprise plan, your organisation Owner must add
AdsWire as a connector first:

1. Go to **Organisation settings → Connectors → Add → Custom → Web**
2. Paste your MCP endpoint URL
3. After the Owner adds it, members click **Connect** in Customize → Connectors

## Step 3 — Connect Google Ads

In your AdsWire dashboard → **Connect Platform** → **Google Ads**
→ Sign in with your Google account → grant access → done.

Connect your Manager Account (MCC) to give AdsWire access to all
your client accounts in one step.

## Step 4 — Ask your first question

In a new Claude conversation:

```
Show me all my Google Ads accounts
```

Then try the four-prompt agency diagnostic:

```
1. Show me all disapproved ads for account [customer_id]
2. Show me keywords wasting money with no conversions
3. What search terms are triggering my ads but not converting?
4. Add the suggested negatives to the campaign
```
